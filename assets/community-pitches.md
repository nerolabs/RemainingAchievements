The single best-targeted audience: the opposite-faction feature is built for
exactly this crowd. Post in their self-promo / #community-creations channel.

**Draft message:**

> Hey all — I built a small (free, MIT) WoW addon that I think fits this
> community well, especially for two-faction completionists. **Remaining

>

> filter and a one-click **spreadsheet export** that pastes straight into
> Sheets/Excel (ID, name, category, points, Wowhead link, faction flags).

> CurseForge: <CF link> — feedback very welcome; a lot of the features started
> as community requests. Not trying to spam, just thought it'd be genuinely
> useful here. 🙂
