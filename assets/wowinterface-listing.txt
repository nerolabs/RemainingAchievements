--- Title ---
Remaining Achievements

--- Short description (one-liner) ---
Adds a "Remaining" tab to the Achievement UI: every incomplete achievement in one searchable, filterable list — stash, hidden, Feats of Strength, opposite-faction, and spreadsheet export. For completionists & achievement hunters.

--- Description (BBCode) ---
[b]Ever wondered what's actually left?[/b] The default Achievement UI makes you click through every category to find what you haven't done. Remaining Achievements adds a fourth tab — [b]Remaining[/b] — that lists every incomplete achievement on your account in one flat, scrollable, searchable list. A proper achievement tracker for completionists and achievement hunters.

[i]Many of the features below started as community requests and bug reports — thank you, and please keep the feedback coming.[/i]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]One list, all categories.[/b] Native Blizzard achievement rows: icon, points shield, tooltips, click to expand objectives, shift-click to link in chat. Progressive chains show their later steps too, not just the current one.
[*][b]Search[/b] by name or description.
[*][b]Filter by category.[/b] A dropdown of checkboxes (all on by default) lets you hide whole top-level categories — PvP, Pet Battles, Delves, Housing, whatever you don't chase — from the list, the counts, and the export.
[*][b]Stash for later.[/b] Click the X on a row (or right-click) to set aside an achievement you'll never do. "Show stashed for later (N)" reveals them again with one-click restore. Persists account-wide.
[*][b]Hidden achievements.[/b] A toggle surfaces the point-carrying achievements Blizzard hides from the UI until they're earned.
[*][b]Feats of Strength — obtainable only[/b] [i](beta)[/i]. A toggle shows the feats you can still actually earn, current season included; retired promotions, old seasonal rewards, realm firsts, and dead one-time events are filtered out.
[*][b]Opposite faction.[/b] Log a character of your other faction and open the tab once — after that, a toggle shows the achievements only that faction can still earn, tagged (Horde)/(Alliance), with combined "(+N Horde)" totals in the header. Built for DataForAzeroth-style completionists who track both sides.
[*][b]Spreadsheet export.[/b] One click builds a table (ID, name, category, points, description, reward, Wowhead link, stashed/hidden/faction flags). Copy it, then paste directly into Google Sheets or Excel — proper columns, no import step.
[*][b]Right-click menu[/b] on any row: stash/restore, track on the objectives HUD, link to chat.
[*][b]Localized[/b] in 10 languages — German, Spanish (EU & LatAm), French, Italian, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, and Chinese (Simplified & Traditional) — with English fallback for anything untranslated.
[*][b]Plays nice with ElvUI[/b] — the tab picks up your skin.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Usage[/b][/size]
Open achievements (Y) and click the [b]Remaining[/b] tab. That's it.

[size=4][b]Notes[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]"Remaining" uses the same account-wide completion semantic as the default UI.
[*]The Feats of Strength toggle [i](beta)[/i] is filtered from game data that doesn't track obtainability — if something unobtainable slips through (or something real is missing), please report it.
[*]Retail only; guild achievements are out of scope.
[*]Bugs and requests: [url]https://github.com/nerolabs/RemainingAchievements/issues[/url]
[/list]

Whether you're chasing a DataForAzeroth two-faction clear, hunting your last few achievement points, or just want to see what's left — Remaining Achievements puts it all on one screen.
